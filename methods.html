METHODS (version 0.2.0)

Reopen Ledger publishes three things about Coinbase-issued tokenized stocks on Base:

  1. Calls on the record. Anyone can put a forecast on the record in a public contract before a window opens; every forecast is scored against the Chainlink reference feed after the window closes.
  2. The weekend tape. While the reference feed is frozen (nights are not frozen; weekends and market holidays are), the tokens keep trading on DEXs. The tape measures how far the DEX price moves from the frozen reference, and how close the weekend DEX price comes to the value the reference shows at reopen.
  3. What these markets can absorb. Pool depth below the current price, set against the collateral that lending markets on Base would liquidate if the reference moved down 5%, 10% or 20%.

Every published number is a measurement with its inputs. Nothing here is a verdict on any token, pool, protocol, issuer or forecaster, and nothing here says what anyone should do in any market.

Every export is stamped with methods_version. A change to any definition below bumps the version.

1. Principles

  • Measurements, not verdicts. Flags are booleans with a heuristic id and an evidence pointer. Tiers (no_flags, one_flag, flagged, stale, insufficient_data) count flags; they assert nothing else.
  • Replayable. Every published file is a pure function of the raw chain logs in data/raw/, the config files in config/ and this document. callrec replay regenerates an export from those inputs and compares it file by file. An export is as of the earliest time (whole minutes) to which every raw stream has been read, never later; the manifest records how far each stream was read (data_read_to_ms). Rows read later can then never change a replay. The daily export is refused when a stream lags the requested time by more than 2 hours.
  • Signed and anchored. Each export's manifest is signed with the key in keys/export-key.json, and the SHA-256 of the manifest is put on the record on Base once a day (schema 3, section 4).
  • House wallets are labelled and excluded. The project's own forecasters commit from the five wallets in config/house.yaml, on the schedule in section 7, one transaction per forecaster per window. No usage figure ever counts them.
  • Open probabilities are shown after resolution. The site shows that a wallet committed to an open window, never its probabilities until the window has resolved. They are public on chain regardless.
  • No positions. The operator holds no position in any measured token, pool or lending market.

Measured tokens. Official tokens with a Chainlink 24/5 reference feed (12 at version 0.1.0). The other official tokens appear in the registry only.

Pools used. Listed pools of measured tokens that pair with USDC, on Aerodrome Slipstream (both factories), Uniswap v3 and Uniswap v4 (45 pools at version 0.1.0; they carry about 95% of the measured tokens' listed volume). WETH and native-ETH pairs, pairs with other tokens, and pools on factories with a different swap event are not used yet.

Where the swap logs live. The swap streams hold tens of thousands of logs a day, so they are not kept in the repository. They are re-read from the chain (callrec ingest swaps), and the export manifest records the SHA-256 of every day file's uncompressed content (the rows, one JSON object per line, sorted keys, LF endings). Serialisation is deterministic, so a re-read can be checked byte for byte on any machine; the compressed bytes are not hashed, because they depend on the zlib build. Every other raw input is kept in the repository.

Block times come from the block number (config/chain.yaml block_time: 2 s blocks until block 53,556,127, 200 ms blocks from 53,556,128, both checked against headers). The value of a feed at time t is the answer of the latest AnswerUpdated, from any phase aggregator of that feed, whose block time is at or before t. Round ids are never used to order or look up values.

The reference feed already includes the token's multiplier (Chainlink: token price = underlying price x multiplier), so DEX prices of the token compare with it directly.

3. Windows

Times are US Eastern (America/New_York). The 24/5 session of the reference feeds runs from Sunday 20:00 to Friday 20:00; full market holidays (config/calendar.yaml, from the NYSE calendar) close the session that ends at 20:00 on that day.

  • Closed window (kind 1). A closed span runs from a session close to the next reopen: Friday 20:00 to Sunday 20:00, longer around a holiday, or a mid-week holiday span. Its window is dated by the Eastern date the span starts.
  • start: the span start (the reference value then is the frozen value);
  • cutoff: reopen minus 120 minutes;
  • end: reopen plus 60 minutes; the outcome is the reference value then.
  • Daily window (kind 2), one per open session day d: from 20:00 on day d-1 (or from the previous closed window's end) to 20:00 on day d; cutoff = start.

Window id = kind x 100,000,000 + yyyymmdd. A record counts for a window only if its block time is at or before the window's cutoff.

4. Records (the CallLedger contract)

commit(uint32 windowId, uint16 schemaId, bytes data) emits Committed(address indexed committer, uint32 indexed windowId, uint16 indexed schemaId, bytes data) and stores nothing. The contract has no owner, no admin, no storage and no payable function. Bytes after the ABI arguments (ERC-8021 attribution suffixes) are ignored, so a caller's Builder Code rides along.

Schema Meaning Encoding of data
1 probability calls 26 bytes per token, sorted by address: token (20) | p_up_bps uint16 (0 to 10,000) | ret_bps int32 (point call, expected log return in basis points; 0x80000000 = none)
2 coverage call snapshot block uint64, then 74 bytes per token: token (20) | for 5%, 10%, 20%: depth_usd uint48, liquidation_flow_usd uint48, underwater_debt_usd uint48 (whole dollars; 0xFFFFFFFFFFFF = not measured)
3 daily anchor SHA-256 of the day's export manifest (32) | export date yyyymmdd uint32

p_up_bps is the probability, in basis points, that the reference value at the window's end is above its value at the window's start. The first record per (committer, window, schema) is the scored one; later records are kept and shown, never scored.

5. Scoring

Slate. A window's slate is every official token with a 24/5 reference feed that updated at least once in the seven days before the window starts. The slate is fixed from data before the window opens.

Outcome. For slate token k in window W: o = 1 if ref(W.end) > ref(W.start), 0 if below. The token is void for W, with the reason published, when: - either value is missing (missing_reference); - a token event falls inside W: a multiplier update scheduled or applied, a cancellation, an announcement, a pause or unpause (token_event_in_window); - for a closed window, the feed has not updated after the reopen by W.end (no_update_after_reopen); - for a daily window, the end value is more than 26 hours old (stale_reference); - the two values are equal (unchanged_reference).

Brier score. For a forecaster that committed to W before its cutoff: (p - o)^2 per non-void slate token, where a slate token it left out counts as p = 0.5.

Baselines. Computed for every window, whether or not the house committed: - 50%: p = 0.5 everywhere (Brier 0.25). - DEX-implied (section 6): from the gap between the DEX price and the frozen or current reference. - Persistence (point calls only): an expected return of 0.

Per forecaster, over the windows it committed to: - brier: mean Brier over its scored (window, token) pairs; - skill_vs_dex = 1 - brier / (DEX-implied baseline's Brier on the same pairs); - skill_vs_50 = 1 - brier / 0.25 (a forecaster at 50% everywhere scores exactly 0); - edge_lcb: with d_w = mean over W's tokens of (baseline Brier - forecaster Brier), the one-sided 95% lower bound mean(d) - 1.645 x sd(d) / sqrt(n) over its n scored windows.

Ranking. By edge_lcb, for forecasters with at least 5 scored windows; the rest are listed unranked.

What the board cannot show yet. With a few dozen windows, skill and luck cannot be told apart. The lower bound and the minimum count reduce, not remove, the chance that a ranking reflects luck; with many forecasters, the best of them will look skilled by chance. The site says so next to the ranking.

Point calls (ret_bps given): squared error, in bps^2, of the realised log return ln(ref(end)/ref(start)) x 10,000, reported against persistence (0) on the same pairs. Not ranked.

6. The weekend tape and the DEX-implied baseline

For each closed window and token, from the Swap logs of the token's listed pools: - the DEX price path: after each swap, the pool's price in USD (USDC = 1; WETH pairs at the Chainlink ETH/USD value at that time); - the time-weighted DEX price (TWAP) of the primary pool (the listed pool with the most USDC volume over the trailing 30 days) over [span start, cutoff], and its last-hour TWAP before the cutoff; - gap_bps = ln(TWAP_last_hour / frozen reference) x 10,000; - reopen_bps = ln(reference at window end / frozen reference) x 10,000; - tape_error_bps = reopen_bps - gap_bps (how far the weekend market was from the reopen value); - void when the primary pool has fewer than 5 swaps or less than $1,000 of volume in the last 12 hours before the cutoff (dex_thin), or when the last-hour price is more than 30% from the reference (dex_price_outlier: a broken or empty pool).

DEX-implied probability. p = Phi(gap_bps / sigma), clipped to [0.05, 0.95]. Sigma is 1.4826 x the median absolute tape_error_bps over every earlier closed window (all tokens, non-void rows, windows that ended before this window's start). That equals the standard deviation for normal errors, and one broken pool cannot swamp it. Its floor is 25 bps, and the prior is 150 bps until 4 earlier windows exist. Its point call is gap_bps. For daily windows the gap is taken at the cutoff, against the then-current reference, with a separate sigma from earlier daily windows (prior 150 bps). Where the DEX baseline is void the baseline is p = 0.5.

The three 24/7 Chainlink feeds (GOOGL, NVDA, TSLA) are shown on the tape next to the DEX price as a second weekend value; they are not used in any score.

7. House forecasters and schedule

Wallet (role) Records When
null schema 1, p = 0.5 on every slate token daily windows: in the 12 hours before the cutoff; closed windows: from the span start to the cutoff
persistence schema 1, p = 0.5 and a point call of 0 daily windows: in the 12 hours before the cutoff; closed windows: from the span start to the cutoff
dex_implied schema 1, the DEX-implied p and point call (section 6); p = 0.5 and no point call where the DEX baseline is void closed windows: in the 2 hours before the cutoff; daily windows: in the hour before the cutoff
coverage schema 2, the coverage call (section 8) each closed window, within 2 hours after the span start
anchor schema 3, the daily export hash once a day

Each runs from a scheduled job, at most one transaction per role per window (the job reads the chain first and does nothing if the record exists). The job is started at fixed times by scheduled pushes (config/house_ticks.yaml), with GitHub's own schedule as a backstop: GitHub ran it 6 to 8 hours late in the first week, so the null and persistence slots of daily windows are 12 hours wide. A Monday job reads the chain for the past closed window's house records and publishes house_commit_missing (with the expected role and window) if one is absent. House wallets never trade and never hold the measured tokens. They appear on the board, labelled, and are excluded from every usage figure.

8. What these markets can absorb

Depth (per token, from pool state at a block, liquidity providers assumed not to move): - move_1pct_usd, move_2pct_usd: USD paid out by the pools as the price moves down 1% / 2% from each pool's current price; - impact of a token-to-USDC swap of $10,000 and $100,000 in the primary pool (before the pool fee); - absorb_5pct_below_usd[s]: USD the pools pay out between ref x (1 - s) and ref x (1 - s) x 0.95, for s = 5%, 10%, 20%: the depth that would meet liquidation flow at a shocked price.

Concentrated-liquidity pools are walked tick by tick; constant-product pools use the closed form.

Liquidation flow L(s): for every borrower position in a listed lending market that uses the token as collateral, the collateral that becomes liquidatable if the price the market's oracle reads falls by s, valued at the shocked price, using the market's own liquidation threshold, close factor and incentive. While the reference is frozen nothing can be liquidated; the flow arrives at the reopen, at the gap price. - Each token is shocked alone; other prices are unchanged. - Morpho Blue: liquidatable when collateral x price x LLTV < debt; no close factor; seized collateral = min(collateral, debt x LIF / price) with LIF = min(1.15, 1 / (1 - 0.3 x (1 - LLTV))). - Aave V4: only reserves the user enabled as collateral count. Health factor = sum(collateral x price x collateral factor) / debt. The repaid amount restores the target health factor (1.24), or takes the whole debt or reserve under the $1,000 dust rules. The bonus slides with the health factor. The shocked reserve is liquidated first. - Both protocols read the Chainlink 24/5 feeds without a staleness check (docs/research/LENDING.md).

Underwater debt U(s): the debt of positions whose collateral, at the shocked price, is worth less than the debt.

Coverage = depth / L(s) is published with the dollar figures beside it. Coverage below 1.0 means the measured depth in the 5% below the shocked price is smaller than the measured liquidation flow.

Limits. These measure onchain absorption only: no offchain venue, no liquidator inventory, no liquidity added after the snapshot, no other collateral of the same borrower. A lending market whose positions cannot be read is listed with its reported totals as unmeasured, never silently left out.

Scoring the coverage call. Every closed window with a house coverage record: the depth committed at the span start, compared with the depth at the same price levels in the first snapshot taken in the two hours after the reopen (depth_change_pct). When a token's reopen move is 5% or more, the committed liquidation flow is also compared with the collateral seized by Liquidate (Morpho) and LiquidationCall (Aave V4) events in the 24 hours after the reopen. Every miss stays on the page.

Snapshots are taken once a day, in each coverage slot, and in the hour after each reopen. The depth engine was checked against Uniswap's QuoterV2 at one block and agreed to rounding (docs/research/DEPTH-CHECK.md).

9b. Volume shares by heuristic (supporting numbers)

Per token, over the listed pools, since 2026-08-24 and over the last 7 days: the share of USD swap volume carrying each boolean. No address is named.

Id Boolean, per swap
OV1_round_trip_10m the same address takes both directions in the token's pools within 10 minutes (both legs)
OV3_sandwich_leg in one block and pool, an address's swap, then another address's swap in the same direction, then the first address's swap in the other direction (the first address's two legs)
OV2_shared_funding not measured (needs every address's funding graph); published as null

The address of a swap is its recipient, a proxy; an address that receives more than 200 swaps in the token's pools on one UTC day is treated as router-like and left out of OV1. Swaps are grouped by UTC day, so a round trip that crosses midnight UTC is not joined.
