Predict the reopen

Give each stock a probability that it reopens higher than its frozen price. One transaction sends them to a contract that holds no funds; after the window closes, Chainlink's price scores every prediction. Your first prediction per window is the one that counts.

This records a forecast. It does not touch any token, route anything or move any value; the transaction carries no ETH beyond gas. Your probabilities appear on the board only after the window resolves.


No browser wallet? The button opens a Coinbase window that signs you in with a passkey (Face ID or fingerprint) and creates a wallet if you have none. Gas is covered; no app, no seed phrase.

Use the TypeScript client (commitCalls) or send commit(uint32 windowId, uint16 1, bytes data) yourself; the encoding is in METHODS section 4. Append your own Builder Code in the ERC-8021 suffix; it is kept.

House schedule

The project's own forecasters commit once per window (METHODS section 7). Daily windows: null and persistence in the 12 hours before 20:00 ET, DEX-implied in the hour before. Closed spans: null and persistence after the Friday close, DEX-implied in the two hours before the cutoff (Sunday 16:00 to 18:00 ET), and the coverage call within two hours of the close. All house wallets are labelled and excluded from every usage figure.
